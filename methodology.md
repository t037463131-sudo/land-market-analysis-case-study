# Methodology and evidence limits

1. Keep transaction-level and parcel-detail records separate and link them with internal case identifiers.
2. Standardize dates and units; compare reported unit prices with total price divided by transferred area without silently replacing rounded source values.
3. Screen explicit exclusions before assigning core, secondary, and comparison categories.
4. Reconcile transferred area across detail rows, allowing for source rounding. Do not discard rows solely because a parcel identifier repeats.
5. Flag potentially related transactions and compare transaction-level summaries with equal-weight analytical-group summaries.
6. Review sample-size, date-coverage, size, and zoning differences before applying price benchmarks.
7. Keep unresolved document evidence explicit. No identification of a relationship does not prove transaction independence; no exclusion note does not prove complete title or an arm's-length transaction.

This is a retrospective summary of the supplied workbook. Source classifications and grouping judgments were not independently re-established from contracts or official records during portfolio preparation. It is a case study of analytical reasoning, not a property valuation or a reproduction package.
